Multi-Hazard Sensor Combination & Classification Engine

How sensors fuse into multi-hazard warning outputs (Node 1 & Dummy Node Architectures)

Pi Edge AI & ML
🔥 1. Fire / Combustible Gas
Sensors: MQ-2 + BME280 / VOC / PM2.5

Triggered when smoke/gas analog ADC increases concurrently with ambient temperature spike (\(\Delta T\)). Raspberry Pi / Edge ML classifies it as a potential FIRE or toxic gas release.

🌊 2. Flood / Flash Flood
Sensors: Ultrasonic (HC-SR04) + Rain + BME280

Triggered when water clearance decreases rapidly (rising water level) supported by sustained rainfall and elevated relative humidity (\(\text{RH} > 80\%\)).

📳 3. Earthquake / Structural Tremor
Sensors: MPU6050 Acceleration X/Y/Z

Triggered when acceleration vector shifts suddenly (\(\Delta |a| > 2.5\,\text{m/s}^2\)) or sustained seismic tremors/shaking are detected at the node installation.

🌫️ 4. Air Pollution Severity (AQI)
Sensors: PM2.5 + PM10 + CO + NO2 + SO2

Combines coarse & fine particulates with site-specific toxic gases and wind vector to compute real-time AQI and downwind dispersion hotspots.

☀️ 5. Extreme Heat & Thermal Stress
Sensors: Temperature + Humidity + Solar Radiation

Calculates Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) and solar radiation intensity to generate early heatstroke warnings for field crews.

⛰️ 6. Landslide & Slope Instability
Sensors: Soil Moisture + Tilt + Displacement + Rain

Detects saturation-induced slope failure when soil moisture exceeds 75% alongside angular tilt shifts (\(> 2.0^\circ\)) and ground displacement.
